Our Journey

The Story Behind Examyfi

For decades, students aspiring to become doctors and engineers have struggled—not for lack of talent, but for lack of structured guidance, personalized attention, and timely support. Even the most dedicated students often miss crucial opportunities in scholarships, admissions, and strategic preparation.

Examyfi was born from a simple yet powerful vision: What if we could combine decades of proven teaching wisdom with modern technology? What if every student could have a personalized study plan, instant performance feedback, and access to expert mentors—all in their pocket?

We partnered with three legendary institutions—Rehmani30, Al Amin Mission West Bengal, and Mission Awareness Foundation—who collectively bring over 30 years of excellence in NEET and JEE coaching. Together, we've mentored 100+ IIT and AIIMS alumni, and now we're scaling that expertise to reach every aspiring student across India.

Examyfi is not just another EdTech platform. We're a bridge between time-tested pedagogy and cutting-edge technology. We're a support system built by teachers, for students and teachers. We measure our success not in downloads, but in dreams fulfilled.

Our Core Values

🎯

Student-First Always

Every decision starts with one question: "Does this genuinely help students perform better or feel more supported?" If not, we don't do it.

🤝
